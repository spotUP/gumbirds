Cabinet Art Directions

Three looks for the workplace dodger. Every screen below is drawn live at the Neo Geo's native 320 x 224 and shown at 2x. Colours are rounded to the hardware's 5 bits per channel, and each palette stays within the 15 colours plus transparent that one Neo Geo palette holds. Text sits on the 8 x 8 fix layer grid; logos and characters are sprites. Game names are working titles.

Hardware notes: the Neo Geo has no alpha blending, so menu panels are solid and the torch glow is a dither. The sprite grid view also marks the 8 px border that many MVS monitors crop, so the HUD stays inside it.
