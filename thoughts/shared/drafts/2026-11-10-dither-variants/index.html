Dither variants on hazards v4

Same art, same animation, same palettes as hazards v4. Only the transition between neighbouring shades is redrawn, six ways. No style adds a colour. Every image is shown at a whole-number scale; keep the browser zoom at 100 percent, or the dither will blur. Each comparison sheet shows the reference and the six styles at 3x, and all seven at 1x (real cabinet size) in the last cell.

Sky and city backdrop

The v4 scene sky is three flat bands. Each style redraws the two band edges; the edges are lifted by hand into uneven haze banks, so the dither does not sit on a ruler line.

Water

Seven water shades from abyss to cyan skin, plus rocks. Patterns stay fixed to the tile grid while the waves pass through them.

Lava

Seven melt shades and five crust shades. Melt patterns are fixed to the tile grid; the scene light on the buildings is the v4 light.

Spikes

Five steel shades between outline and rim light. On the blades and shafts the pattern rides with the piston, so it does not flicker when a spike thrusts.
