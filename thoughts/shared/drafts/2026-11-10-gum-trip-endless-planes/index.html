Gum Trip endless background planes

Draft for task T-B4b, step 1. Each parallax plane keeps its arena width and is redrawn at the join so that its right end continues into its left end. The sky does not move. Scroll is to the right at the capped speed of 1.0 pixel per frame.

Speeds in pixels per frame: sky 0, clouds back 0.0625, clouds front 0.125, far 0.25, mid A 0.5, mid B 0.75, near 1.0, hazard strip 1.0, airship 0.1 leftward. The animations run at 20 frames per second with 3 pixels of scroll per frame: the speed is true, the motion is coarser than on the machine. They are 9.6 seconds long and jump back at the end (the planes repeat at different distances, so no short animation closes).

Seam measure 1: rows in which the last and the first pixel column of a plane differ, compared with all neighbouring column pairs inside the plane. Seam measure 2 (far, mid A, mid B): the skyline is drawn 64 pixels past the end of the ring; count of those columns that differ from the first 64 columns of the ring, over all 8 animation frames.

Unique 16 x 16 tiles for far, mid A, mid B and near together: ring 694, arena 685; 48 ring tiles are not in the arena set. Columns changed against the arena planes: far 0, 335; mid A 0, 335..351; mid B 0, 335..367; near 332..342, 347..357, 362..383.
