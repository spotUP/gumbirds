Gum Trip endless planes (T-B4b step 1). Ring = arena plane at its arena width with a redrawn join.
Seam measure 1 (all rings): rows in which the last column differs from the first column (the pair that touches at the
  wrap), next to the same count for every interior neighbour pair of the plane (how many interior pairs are at least
  as different, median, largest). A join at a house edge is as different as the other house edges, never 0.
Seam measure 2 (far, midA, midB): the skyline is drawn 64 px past the ring end; count of those 64 pixel columns, over
  all 8 animation frames, that differ from columns 0..63 of the ring. 0 = the picture continues across the join
  exactly as it continues anywhere else.
