# Hazard drafts, round 3 (polish pass on round 2)

Drafts only. Original art, generated by `draw_hazards_v3.py` (`python3.14 -I draw_hazards_v3.py`; `--only AB` for a subset;
`--check <scratch dir>` writes inspection sheets there and nothing here). Open `index.html` to compare with round 2.

Strip: top at screen row 192, 32 px tall, 64x32 tile repeated 5 times, 16 frames, seamless in x and in time
(the simulations assert periodicity). The 8-frame cuts are dropped.

## Files

- `anim_<K>_16f_4x.gif`: 320 px strip plus 56 px of scene above, 4x, real frame timing (rounded to 10 ms).
- `scene_<K>_3x.png`: full 320x224 scene, frame 2, 3x.
- `frames_<K>_1x.png`: build input. 16 frames of the 64x32 tile in one row (1024x32), RGBA, index 0 transparent.
  Palette order is the list below (index 1 first).

## Changes per weak point

1. Lava (fixed inside the tile, partly over the screen): roll boundaries moved to uneven places (8, 19, 43, 55), plate
   speed scales with roll width, body sway has two phase-offset terms, two bubbles of radius 7 and 4 that burst at
   frames 6 and 13, three small blisters at uneven places and frames, two extra floor heat sources without a bubble.
   Crust plates are 3 to 6 px thick with a lit top edge and a highlight on the end that faces the light (one new colour).
   The 64 px period itself still shows over 320 px, most of all the large bubble.
2. Water (fixed): caustic shapes are cel patches cut from the refracted-ray density (two thresholds), so they move
   with the waves. The deep band is darker and holds floor rocks, weed that sways with the swell, and seven bubbles
   that rise from rock vents with buoyancy against drag. Droplets are 2 and 3 px. A three-frame crown stands at each
   breaking event.
3. Spikes (fixed, two iterations): housing is a 5 px riveted sleeve block per piston with a white lip; blades are
   14 rows tall and 14 px wide at the base, with a white rim on the lit edge and a bright rim on the far edge; the
   arc has a 1 px white core, cyan inner halo, violet outer halo (one new colour) and two forks. Iteration 2 raised
   the rest position so more of the retracted blade shows.
4. Fire (partly): cores are cut from a more strongly smoothed field and every band passes a 3x3 majority filter, so
   1-pixel islands and stair-steps are gone. The six sources now differ in strength (0.58 to 1.00), width and
   spacing. Tall and short tongues show, but in some frames the two strongest sources still read as an even pair.
5. Cast light (fixed as a draft): drawn on the scene, not in the tile. Three nested hard shapes: a low band on the
   building face (hand-placed mound per 16 px column), a rim on building vertical edges, a rim on island undersides
   within 44 px of the hazard. Each 16 px column has a level 1 to 3 per frame, taken from the weighted hot-pixel
   count of the hazard column below it. The script prints the level table (4 columns x 16 frames per hazard).

## Extra palette needs for cast light

Three colours added to the city palette: face (62,22,54), rim dim (170,54,46), rim hot (255,150,62).
The city draft uses 7 colours in this region, so they fit the same palette (10 of 15). Two ways to build it:
- palette swap: the lit pixels are separate indices that hold the unlit colours when dark; the game writes 3 palette
  entries per level change. Cheapest, but the whole screen flickers together (no per-column levels).
- extra palette: lit variants of the bottom city tiles under a second palette, chosen per 16 px column. Keeps the
  per-column flicker shown here.

## Timing and tile cost

Frame time in video frames (60 Hz): spikes 4, fire 4, water 6, lava 8. Loop: 64, 64, 96, 128 video frames.
Tile cost per hazard: 8 tiles per frame x 16 frames = 128 tiles (before deduplication). Cast light adds lit variants
of the bottom city tiles if the extra-palette way is chosen.

## Still weak

- Lava: the large bubble repeats at five places on screen at once; a second tile variant or a sprite bubble would fix it.
- Fire: tongue heights vary less than the source strengths suggest in about a third of the frames.
- Water: rocks and weed sit in the lowest rows and are close to the outline colour; they read only at 3x and above.
- Spikes: palette is full (15); no spare slot.
- Cast light is checked against the draft scene only; building edges come from a colour test on that mock.
