Gumbirds: title logo, fix font and HUD drafts

Draft round, 10 November 2026. Every image is 320 x 224 game pixels shown at 3x unless marked 1x; click an image for full size. Backdrops are earlier draft renders. Costs are in numbers.txt, choices in README.md.

Title logo: three options

A. Stretched gum (sprites)

Sprites: 79 unique 16x16 tiles still, 155 with the 10 frame animation; at most 19 sprites on a scanline; 1 palette of 7 colours.

Weak point: the most expensive option, and pink gum on the pink ember dusk sky keeps its shape only through the dark keyline.

Sheet at 1x:

B. Enamel rooftop sign (fix tiles)

Fix tiles: 174 unique 8x8 tiles in 204 cells; 0 sprites; 1 fix palette of 15 colours; the bulb chase is a 3 entry palette rotation.

Weak point: a flat rectangle that hides a 28 by 9 cell block of sky; the least gum-like of the three and its only motion is the bulb chase.

Sheet at 1x:

C. Floating soot letters (sprites)

Sprites: 16 (8 units of 32x64), 16 on a scanline; 58 unique 16x16 tiles still, 92 with bubble sway frames; 3 palettes, 2 shared with the Sootling gum.

Weak point: the letters are small (23 px) and the ragged soot edge reads as noise at 1x; the least legible from across a room.

Sheet at 1x:

Fix font: two options

Font A, Gumdrop

49 fix tiles plus 20 for the 8x16 digits; 5 text palettes of 15 colours.

Weak point: two pixel stems leave tight counters, so 0, O and D, and M and W, are close at 1x.

Sheets at 1x and at 4x:

Font B, Signwriter

49 fix tiles; same 5 palettes.

Weak point: a close relative of font A; the one pixel right stem is weaker over bright sky and it has no tall digits.

HUD: two layouts

Storm shows the one player case with the join prompt. Both layouts keep to fix rows 0, 1 and 27; the play ceiling is y = 16.

Layout 1, Roof strip

Fix rows 0, 1 and 27; opaque band on rows 0 and 1 from 20 sprites of one dark tile, or 49 more filled-background tiles.

Weak point: the band needs 20 sprites (or 49 more tiles) and the bottom line sits unbacked on the hazard strip.
