Gumbirds islands, draft round 1

View at 100 percent browser zoom. Drafts only, nothing is in the game. Three options for the platforms, each drawn at the eight positions and widths of the stage table in the code, camera at 32 px so both seam islands show. The backdrop is the camera 0 picture of the final background pass, so the parallax offset is not exact. A player Sootling stands on the centre island, a computer Sootling flies above it. Numbers are in numbers.txt, own choices in README.md.

Side by side, 1x (true size), all three moods

A Roof chunk

A brick roof fragment torn loose, stone coping on top, torn brickwork and rebar below, a lantern on a chain under the right end.

Weak point: the torn lower edge is an even sawtooth and the brick face reads flat at true size; only the right end has motion.

41 unique tiles, 1 palette on screen, 17 platform sprites on the worst line.

B Garden slab

A floating planter: bright turf line, soil with buried stones, a root ball, vines and grass that move in the wind.

Weak point: the buried stones and blossoms repeat every 32 px and show as a pattern; it costs the most tiles, and the storm soil sits close to the building greys.

72 unique tiles, 1 palette on screen, 17 platform sprites on the worst line.

C Airship deck

A steel deck plate with a hazard band on a see-through truss, held up by a rotor pod under each end.

Weak point: the backdrop shows through the open truss, so the lower half reads less solid, and it is the option closest in material to the city scenery; the rotors are small at true size.

43 unique tiles, 1 palette on screen, 17 platform sprites on the worst line.
