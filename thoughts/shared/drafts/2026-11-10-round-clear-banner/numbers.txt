Text cells = cells holding a glyph or icon tile. Fix cells = every cell the banner writes (and must clear after).
Contrast = per text cell, mean luminance of the two lightest face colours minus the brightest quarter of what shows
around the glyph in that cell (0..255 scale). Under 90 is hard to read at 1x.
