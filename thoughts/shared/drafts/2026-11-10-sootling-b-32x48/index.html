Sootling at 32 x 48 with the two-lobed gum bubble (drafts, three takes on design B)

View at 100 percent browser zoom. Drafts only, nothing here is game data. All three takes are the same concept: one gum membrane with two lobes blown from the mouth; first pop pulls it round into one bubble; second pop: fall. They differ in body design and in how the membrane is drawn. Every animation runs on four crops of the real backdrops: player on the dusk sun, computer on storm, player on clear night, computer on dusk buildings.

Old 16 x 32 art beside the new 32 x 48 takes (same scale, 4x)

Left group: the sheet in the game now (player, computer), then the previous round design B at 16 x 32 (player, computer, one lobe). Right group: take A, B, C, each as player with two lobes, computer with two lobes, player with one lobe. Rows: ember dusk, clear night, storm.

Take A: Pilot: goggle on the eye, visor cap, candy gum

Widest, roundest body. One big ember goggle, strap round the head, visor cap. Gum drawn as solid glossy candy: dark keyline all round, deep violet inner line on the shadow side, four pink bands, arc highlight, bounce light at the lower left. Nothing shows through.

Weak points:

  • The cap and the goggle cover most of the head: it reads as a pilot first and as a soot creature second.
  • The far lens is a 2 px sliver and can read as noise.
  • Candy gum is opaque: no translucency at all, the least gum-like material of the three.
  • Body and membrane shapes come from formulas (ellipse, fused circles) with hand-placed tufts, face, cap and feet on top. The shading bands are computed, so the clusters are less hand-made than final art needs; a hand clean-up pass is still due.
  • The body fills only the lower third of the 32 x 48 box (about 20 x 17 px); the membrane takes the rest.
  • Tile cost is high: about 36 unique 16 x 16 tiles for the 7-frame build set and about 300 for the extended set. Flips save almost nothing because the light comes from one side.
  • The dark keyline (luminance 10) still sits close to the darkest sky patches (luminance about 21): the weakest tenth of the outline has a step of 14 to 19 on clear night and storm.
  • Pop frames 3 to 6 leave a few loose single pixels from the snapping strands. Fall has 6 frames only.
  • The palette swap changes 14 of 15 indices (fur and ember change too, not only the gum).

Take B: Bright eyes: big eyes, goggles pushed up on the cap, glass gum

Tall ball body with two big white eyes (pupil, glint, lid), the goggles sit on the cap. Gum drawn as glass: dark keyline only on the shadow half, coloured line and a pale inner rim on the lit half, a see-through checker window at the lower left of each lobe where the backdrop shows.

Take C: Fuzzball: glowing ember eyes, small tilted cap with bobble, thin-film gum

Squat spiky soot ball, no whites: two glowing ember eyes in dark sockets, a small cap tilted to the back with a bobble. Gum drawn as a thin film: thick saturated rim, pale dithered centre shifted toward the light (the way a real gum bubble thins out), two small gloss ticks.
