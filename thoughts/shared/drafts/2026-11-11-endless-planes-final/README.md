# Endless planes, final set (Gum Trip loops)

Loop versions of the approved final backgrounds (`../2026-11-10-backgrounds-final/`). Generated by
`draw_endless_planes_final.py`, a copy of the final script with an endless section appended (run it with no
arguments; `--final` runs the original final output instead). Gallery: `index.html`. Measurements: `numbers.txt`.

Import: `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.14 -I tools/import_bg_sheet.py moon --drafts thoughts/shared/drafts/2026-11-11-endless-planes-final`
(importer not run here; the importer's own widths and slot map still assume the old widths).

## Method

Every city plane is drawn as a ring: each building is drawn at its home x and again at +P and -P (P = loop width),
on a canvas P + 96 wide, and the plane is columns 0..P-1. The haze cluster strips are cut to P and repeated. Far
towers keep the light side of their home position in each wrap copy. Near is drawn on a canvas that wraps in x.
Proof: the ring canvas drawn 64 px past the loop end equals columns 0..63 in all 8 animation frames, 0 of 64 columns
differ, far, midA and midB. Animation frame counts and timing are unchanged (sky 4, city and near 8).

## What changed, per plane

- Far, 336 (unchanged width). Same 17 towers. Only columns 0 and 335 change: the wrap copy of the last and first tower
  closes the join. The join falls on the edge between tower 16 (310..335) and tower 0 (0..19).
- Middle A, 352 (was 336). New narrow glass tower with a stepped roof at 338..351, roof line 100. It is taller than
  its neighbours, so it covers the 4 px of building 0 that hang past x 0; the join falls on its right edge. Redrawn
  columns 0 and 334..351 (334..335: the wrap copy and the new tower overlap the old last building's overhang).
- Middle B, 368 (was 336). New deco block with chimneys at 336..367, roof line 138. Join on its right edge, next
  to building 0. Redrawn columns 336..367 only.
- Near, 384 (was 320). Fifth roof of ashlar block at 324..379 (8 px courses, joints staggered by half a block,
  hand-placed lit block edges, flat arches with keystones over the windows, drain pipe), with its own tank, antenna
  and fan, and its own hand cast-light table. A 4 px alley at 380..383 is the join. Redrawn columns 320..383 only.
- Sky, clouds front, clouds back, airship, beams: no change. Sky is static; the others have empty edge columns.

Outside the redrawn ranges every plane equals the approved art in role indices, 0 differences in every frame.

## Colours

The approved sheets are not on the 5-bit grid. These sheets snap every palette exactly as `tools/import_bg_sheet.py`
`snap5` does, so all sheets here are on the grid (0 off-grid colours) and equal what the importer would make from the
approved art. No colours merged in the unchanged planes.
