Hot Gum carrier bubble: three drafts

Drafts only, nothing is in the game. One shared palette of 13 colours (limit 15). Arena frame: build/test-snap/playing.png (moon mood, 12 Nov); the bubble is pasted on the player bird by eye. Normal balloons are pink (player) and green (rivals); the hot gum is orange to white-hot with a magenta shadow edge.

A: Molten wad on the head, with steam

1 sprite column, 16x32 (2 tiles), 12 frames. A blister swells, bursts into flecks, the wad sags with long drips, springs back; two steam puffs rise the whole time. Sits on the crown, under the balloon knot.


B: Angry bubble riding above the balloon

1 sprite column, 16x16 (1 tile), 10 frames from 6 cells. Swells, strains white-hot with eyes shut and trembles, snaps flat, rebounds tall, settles. Highest above the crowd, so the carrier reads first; the face says "bad thing to hold". Tremble and bob are sprite position changes.


C: See-through hot shell around the bird

2 sprite columns, 32x32 (2 tiles each), 9 frames from 4 cells. The bird sits inside a thin glowing membrane that wobbles wide and tall; gloss top left, dithered shade bottom right. Biggest on screen, but wings and feet poke out and two carriers touching would overlap shells.


Sprite cost

Party plan fact 5: 14 gameplay sprites per line, 4 fliers on one line = 8. A and B add 1 on the carrier's lines (9), C adds 2 (10). Not measured in the game (no test-sprite-peak run).
