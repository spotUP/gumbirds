# Guard flashlight: overhand grip

## Verdict

Page `design/brawl-test-v5.html` tile check: **PASS** (hero 7 tiles, worst 12 colours per tile, 0 over).
Laser now leaves the raised lens at head height (lens buffer row 4/5 -> y 148/149, was ~158) and still ends on `HIT_AT` (drone eye).

## What changed

Shared upper body (rows 0-20) redrawn once by hand (`scratchpad/grip/upper.txt`) and applied by `scratchpad/grip/grip.py`
to every frame whose head/body matched the old forward gun pose; each frame keeps its own legs (rows 21+).

| Frames | Change |
|---|---|
| run 0-5 | front arm raised: shoulder pad -> upper arm angled down to elbow (rows 10-14) -> forearm up -> steel fist at shoulder/ear height (rows 6-9). Flashlight lies over the fist: dark navy tube (N/L/D + one A/W specular), 2 px steel head flared 1 px above/below, lens col 32 (c Y Y c). Length 11 px (tail col 22 to lens col 32), tube 4 px thick. Old rear-hand glove and under-barrel hand removed, chest redrawn as uniform. Free (rear) arm swings: forearm+glove shift 0,-1,-2,-2,-1,0 px |
| run_shoot 0-5 | same as run + bezel interior glows cyan (col 31 rows 4-5 = c) |
| run_recoil 0-5 | as run_shoot + torch and fist kick back 1 px toward the shoulder (rows 1-7); page fire frame = run_recoil 2 |
| aim_forward, duck, jump (+ recoils) | same template; jump free arm flung back 2 px; recoils kicked like run_recoil |
| hit, aim_diag_up, aim_up, aim_diag_down, aim_down | **unchanged** (still the earlier two-hand hold) |

Sheet `assets/hero/guard_sheet.png/.json`: same packing, all rects identical to before (verified), sheet 218x612.
PNG round-trips to the ASCII frames exactly. CREDITS.md: one line added under "Changes made".

## Colours

Guard uses 14 colours (ABDGKLNRSWYbcs), the existing GUARD palette; none added.
Worst 16x16 tile across the whole sheet: 13 colours (aim_up tile 0,0, unchanged frame); worst in re-posed frames 12. Limit 15.

## Open doubts

- The four aim angles and the hit frame still show the old two-hand hold. Overhand grip for aim_diag_down (classic cop pose, beam down-forward) needs the legs/gun region redrawn; aim_up/aim_diag_up arguably stay overhead holds. Needs a decision.
- Gloves stay steel (same as the torch head); the fist separates from the tube by the dark tube colour and ink, not by hue. A skin or dark glove would read stronger but touches every frame.
- Free-arm swing is small (max 2 px) because only the rear forearm moves; the upper arm stays fixed.
- Fire frame lens is high (head height), so the beam is near-flat (climb 4-5 px over ~208 px). Looks right in the render, but it now passes close to roof props at that height in other layouts.
- Viewed at 6-10x by eye; not yet checked on a real 1x CRT/MiSTer.
