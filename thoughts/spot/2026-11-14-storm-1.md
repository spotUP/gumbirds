# STORM-1: storm background mood

## What changed

- **Measurement**: the storm planes cost the same sprites per line as moon on every line (plane max 65 columns).
  The art was never over budget. The overage came from the budget model: the storm entry had `rain=1`, and the
  rain row (FG_RAIN_SPRITE, 10 per line, lines 0..224) gave 96 of 90 at line 112. The older "91 of 90" figure was
  from an old draft model. Rain is not built, and its slots hold the arcade stage elements
  (`ARC_ELEM_SPRITE == FG_RAIN_SPRITE`), so counting rain there counts those slots twice.
- **What was cut**: nothing in the art. Storm is built with rain 0. The sprite_budget.c comment says B6 rain must
  bring per-line rows, not a flat 10.
- **Importer**: `tools/import_bg_sheet.py` storm MOODS entry (backdrop #080810, trip 66,123,123), new
  `assets/storm/storm_sheet.{json,png}` from the relit drafts. Moon and dusk outputs are unchanged.
- **Build**: `tools/build_storm_sprites.py`, Makefile storm rule, `gen/storm` in CROM_SETS after dusk.
- **Game**: `GAME_MOOD_STORM = GAME_MOOD_COUNT`, `BG_MOOD_COUNT` 3 (src/core/game.h). GAME_MOOD_COUNT stays the
  rotation (moon, dusk). bg.c loads the storm palettes at boot and fills the storm strip pairs. vram_layout.h puts
  STORM_TILE_BASE and STORM_PAL_SLOT after dusk; soot follows storm.
- **Tests**: test_bg.c checks the storm tables against moon geometry. test-mood now forces storm (probe entry 28 =
  Game mood) and counts every sprite in VRAM per line for 120 frames.
