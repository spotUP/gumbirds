## Arena rule
Mood (period 2, game_next_mood), look (period 3, matches_won mod 3) and hazard (period 8, round order list) each change every round, never the same value twice in a row. Each hazard kind falls once on an even and once on an odd round, so 24 rounds show all 2 x 3 x 4 combinations. Fire stays in the mix.

## Bonus stage
The bonus runs no `match_start`: it keeps the cleared stage's sky and look (its layout has no islands). Its hazard is now the cleared stage's world hazard (before: next kind in the round cycle). The CLEAR before a plain stage already loads the next stage's world hazard.
