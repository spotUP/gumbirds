# PLAY-BUGS report

Branch `fix/play-bugs` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/play-bugs`), from ad8ee21.

## 1. BONUS-FLOOR-LATE: fixed

Cause: the bonus floor uses the island sprite slots. `bonus_static_place()` (src/main.c) places the floor once and then only
when `g_cam_px` changes. The same frame the arcade state becomes BONUS, `stage_select(ARC_LAYOUT_BONUS)` (game.c) makes
main.c call `island_relayout()`. On the next frame `island.c load_step()` with `g_load_i == 0` parks every island slot
(`gfx_scb_run(ADDR_SCB4 + ISLAND_SPRITE, 1, g_scb4, ISLAND_COUNT)`, x = SCREEN_W). The bonus layout has 0 island columns,
so nothing writes those slots again until the camera moves, that is, until the player moves.

Fix: `island_loaded()` (src/island.[ch]) returns `g_loaded`. `bonus_static_place()` returns with `g_bonus_cam = -1` while
the island load is not finished, so the floor is placed fully once the load ends (about 9 frames, under the banner).

Check: tests/mame/arcade_check.lua (test-arcade-bonus) keeps the inputs off for the first 16 bonus frames, then needs at
least one island-slot column with an SCB3 height and an on-screen SCB4 x.
- Before (HEAD main.c, new check): `FAIL bonus floor not on screen 16 frames into the bonus (no island-slot column with a height and an on-screen x)`.
- After: see Gates.

## 3. GRAB-GAP: fixed

Cause: grabber element y was 188 (src/core/arc_elements.c stage 3 and stage 6 rows, comment "drain y = water line 188" from
the old gen3 layout). arc_view.c draws the hand bottom at `e->y`. The drawn water surface is
`HAZARD_KILL_WATER - HAZARD_DIP` = 192 + 12 = 204 (src/core/hazard_kind.h). Gap: 16 px.

Fix: `ARC_GRAB_Y (HAZARD_KILL_WATER - HAZARD_DIP)` in arc_elements.h; both grabbers use it. The bite box and the low band
(`ARC_GRAB_LOW_DY`) follow `e->y`, so they move with it.

Note: stage 7 (N08) is a lava world (lava surface 202). The hand there now sits 2 px under the lava surface: no gap.

## 4. GRAB-MISS: fixed

Cause, two parts:
- The bite box is `e->y - 38 .. e->y`. With y 188 it ended 16 px above the water: a flier skimming the water (centre
  188..204) passed under it. Fixed by bug 3.
- While rising, the hand kept a target only within 16 px and slid at most 4 px a tick (`ARC_GRAB_TRACK_DX`,
  `ARC_GRAB_FOLLOW`). Reference (thoughts/spot/2026-11-13-grabber-trigger.md, line 12): the fish teleports to the target
  x while rising and retreats only when the target leaves the low band, leaves the x range or is gone.

Fix (src/core/arc_elements.c): `grab_target` returns the nearest low flier inside the hand's x range `[x0, param]`.
While rising, the hand jumps to that flier's x; no flier low in range means retreat. `ARC_GRAB_TRACK_DX` and
`ARC_GRAB_FOLLOW` are removed (no other users). Rival avoid (`arc_elements_avoid_dir`): while the hand rises (timer
1..ARC_GRAB_RISE), a low rival anywhere over the range (plus half width and pad) steers away; otherwise the old box at the
hand x.

Check: tests/host/test_arc_elements.c. The y values are now relative to `ARC_GRAB_Y`. New cases: the hand jumps to a
target 1 px over the surface and bites it at frame 5; a target outside the range makes it retreat; `ARC_GRAB_Y` equals
the water line. Before (old arc_elements.c, new test): 8+ FAIL lines, first `test_arc_elements.c:33: a.el[i].y ==
pos[s][i].y`. After: `test_arc_elements: 8179 checks, 0 failures`.
