## Method

Temporary `volatile u8 g_prof_mark` in `src/main.c`, written at 12 points in
`game_loop`; a MAME Lua tap (`prof_tap.lua`, kept in the session scratchpad
only) reads the raster line at each mark write and reports mean/max per
section per stage. Run through `gmake test-arcade-walk`. Note: the old walk
recipe greps output with `^ARC_\|BRAWL_TEST`, which hid all `PROF` lines; that
is why the earlier walk-hunt agent saw no profile output.

The profiled walk PASSed with steady maxima identical to the unprofiled run,
so the tap does not change the timing it measures.

The rise is general (every stage, including 1, 2 and 9), so the cause is a

Those stages only hit the ceiling because they started closer to it.

Section split (mean / max lines):

- Stage 3: flow_step 72.3 / 160.4, hero_and_mode 63.4 / 83.2,
  arcade_elems 16.3 / 19.1. Worst frame 223.7 = flow_step 136.1 +
  hero_and_mode 75.4 + arcade_elems 17.2 + rest.
- Stage 10: flow_step 71.2 / 140.3, arcade_elems 33.7 / 36.5,
  hero_and_mode 58.3. Worst frame 224.0 = flow_step 133.5 + hero 58.3 +
  elems 33.2.
- Stage 1: flow_step 55.1 / 98.1; worst frame 173.4 = flow_step 89.7 +
  hero_and_mode 65.9 + elems 16.7.
- Worst frames on stages 3-12 have flow_step 126-141 lines against 90-101 on
  stages 1-2.
- Small sections: hazard select + view + sound about 13; gum + splash 5-11;
  island_step mean 6.7 with rare 60-76 spikes; bg_update 0.7 mean, rare 59.5
  on stages 5 and 9 (mood change frame, not steady).

Conclusion so far: the extra cost is inside `game_step_arena`
(src/core/game.c, called as flow_step), and its worst frames scale with stage
content (rivals, elements). hero_and_mode is large (60-85) but was not
compared against 11-13 and may also have grown.
